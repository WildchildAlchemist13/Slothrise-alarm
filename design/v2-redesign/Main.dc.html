SLOTHRISE · REDESIGN V2

Make getting up feel like a small win, not a fight.

WHO · THE SERIAL SNOOZER “I set five alarms. I’m still late.” Needs an alarm they can’t dismiss half-asleep. Hates feeling judged for it.
WHO · THE ROUTINE BUILDER “I want my first hour to count.” Needs momentum right after waking, and progress they can see.
WHO · THE LIGHT SLEEPER “A loud alarm ruins my whole morning.” Needs a soft wake that still works when they don’t.
Proto-personas: assumptions to check in 5 short interviews before launch.

The morning, step by step

Bedtime Pain: fiddly setup when tired.
Do: one-tap “same as usual”.
The ring Pain: panic, groping for snooze.
Do: one huge, calm action.
Getting up Pain: back to sleep in 2 min.
Do: a mission that needs a brain.
First 30 minutes Pain: phone scroll eats the hour.
Do: hand over to the routine.
During the day Pain: no reason to open the app.
Do: streak on the widget, not in-app.
The dot shows mood: lowest at the ring, highest after the routine. The design’s job is to shorten the dip.

Design principles

Half-asleep proof. One action per wake screen, 64px targets, big type.
Follow the day. Warm and light by morning, dim and quiet by night.
Snooze leads, UI follows. The mascot carries personality; the interface stays calm.
Show the cost, never shame. Snooze says what it costs before you tap it.
Earned colour. Sky gradients only for the ring, sunrise and win.
Tactile, like the art. Plum outlines and buttons that press down, matching Snooze.
